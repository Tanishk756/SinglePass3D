RECONSTRUCTION ENGINE

Evidence at every stage.

01

Capture intelligence

Reject blur and exposure failures. Measure overlap, feature strength, motion, and planar-scene risk before costly processing.

02

Visual geometry

Register camera poses with sequential feature matching and bundle adjustment. Dynamic pixels can be masked automatically.

03

Metric alignment

Synchronize GPS and flight metadata, reject position outliers, then transform geometry into a local metric ENU frame.

04

Dense delivery

Fuse multi-view stereo depth, clean the observed cloud, create a colored surface, and export PLY, OBJ, GLB, JSON, and GeoJSON.
